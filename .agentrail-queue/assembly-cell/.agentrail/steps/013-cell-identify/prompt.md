Saga 17 step 13 (the user, 2026-11-24: 'a way to look at a component and recognize its label/value (e.g., resistor color bands, or some ID on a capacitor)'; docs/plan.md C12). Every part instance carries its markings as data: a resistor's value as 4- or 5-band colors, a capacitor's code (104, 10uF 25V), an IC's part number and pin-1 notch, a transistor's type, an LED's color, a diode's band. The models draw them (bands; printed text as a texture on the body). identify PART turns the part (or moves the microscope) to its marked side and reads it: oracle mode gives the marking and the value it decodes to; sensed mode decodes the microscope's picture -- band colors sampled from the rendered image, printed text matched against the cell's own glyphs (no outside OCR) -- with a confidence, and can fail on a hidden side, glare or blur, so the agent must turn the part. A decoder library in Rust (resistor color code, EIA capacitor codes) with tests; an MCP tool; a misread or unreadable part is a warning, never silently right.
