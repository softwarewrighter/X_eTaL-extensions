A frame of steel wire made with the wire feeder, bender and welder (or a ready-made frame part), parts welded or soldered to it by tabs, the frame held by an arm or the vise and turned and moved as one rigid subassembly; late in the program trim cuts the tabs and the frame is set aside. Supports marked as such, drawn in their own color, left out of the netlist (the check fails while any remains); the current's path through a conductive frame counted in the weld risk. A demo that builds on a frame, turns it over, and cuts it away; tests. Read docs/plan.md (C16) and docs/cell-design.md.
