Saga 17 step 21 (inserted 2026-11-25, decision C19, the user: 'a rig with pins in slots ... an arm places a component's lead into this rig between the pins, and some pins move in opposite directions to force a bend. This only works on one plane. The same component flipped over could have its 2nd lead bent along the same plane, or if rotated along its axis the other lead could have an orthogonal bend. Such a bent leads component can then be trimmed, and moved to the soldering area to connect to a prior component'). A bending rig: a fixed fixture with pins in slots beside the turntable; the arm holds the part and places a lead between the pins at a distance from the body; the pins move in opposite directions and bend the lead round one of them in the rig's plane (the angle, the mandrel's radius); the arm flips the part or turns it about its axis for the other lead or an orthogonal bend; then the cutter trims, and the part goes to the soldering area to be joined to the work. Commands, collision, the rig drawn with its pins moving; tests; a program that forms a resistor's leads into a U or an L in the rig, trims them and solders it to the work; published.
