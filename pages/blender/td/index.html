X_eTaL Extensions

Tower defense pieces

The twelve pieces X_eTaL-games' tower defense asked for, each made by an X_eTaL program driving Blender (game-assets.xtl, the pieces in Td.xtl). Drag a piece to turn it; the buttons play its clips. Each is a .glb (glTF 2.0) with its clips and a preview .png; manifest.toml lists them with their SHA-256s, the license (MIT) and the commit that made them.
