What this is

A city worked out as arrays by an X_eTaL program -- 240 lots, each tower's height, footprint and skin a hash of the lot's number and the seed -- and written as the calls of a player. Blender's player ray-traces the same calls into a film; this player draws them with Three.js as you move.

Against the film: the puddles mirror the city by drawing it a second time upside down, not by tracing rays, so screens and lamps do not light the walls; nothing casts a shadow; the haze is a fade with distance and the steam a few flat sheets; the windows and puddles are the same rules with other noise, so they fall in other places.
