programextensions/audio/demos/scope.xtl

A live oscilloscope: the synthesizer's music (Instruments.xtl) plays while a window draws the samples under the playhead -- the left channel above, the right below -- each frame, triggered on the left channel's rising zero crossing so a steady chord stands still. Run: just demo audio scope (q or close: quit)
