programextensions/audio/demos/spectrum.xtl

The music visualizer: an Ogg Vorbis (or MP3, WAV) file plays while X_eTaL turns what is playing into a spectrum and the spectrum into 16 spokes in a 3D window -- Rust decodes, plays and draws; the analysis and the picture's geometry are X_eTaL arrays. Run: just demo audio spectrum (Space: pause, j/k: back/on 5 s, q: quit) Another file: change the path below (Ogg Vorbis, MP3 or WAV).
