libraryextensions/audio/lib/Audio.xtl

Audio.xtl -- the facade of the audio extension: decode Ogg Vorbis, MP3 and WAV, play them on the sound device, read what is playing as arrays, and play arrays. Import: "au:" u_se< "Audio" Run with xetal-x (xetal-x --ext DIR puts this directory on XETAL_PATH). With XETAL_HEADLESS=1 (or XETAL_AUDIO=off) nothing sounds and playback advances a 60th of a second per au:w_indow (or au:p_layed) -- for tests. Each signature line is written out by the binding macro (lib/Ffi.xtlm).
