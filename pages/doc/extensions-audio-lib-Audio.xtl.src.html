1⍝# Audio.xtl -- the facade of the audio extension: decode Ogg Vorbis, MP3 2⍝# and WAV, play them on the sound device, read what is playing as arrays, 3⍝# and play arrays. 4⍝# Import: "au:" u_se< "Audio" 5⍝# Run with xetal-x (xetal-x --ext DIR puts this directory on XETAL_PATH). 6⍝# With XETAL_HEADLESS=1 (or XETAL_AUDIO=off) nothing sounds and playback 7⍝# advances a 60th of a second per au:w_indow (or au:p_layed) -- for tests. 8⍝# Each signature line is written out by the binding macro (lib/Ffi.xtlm). 9 10ᶠᶠⁱ⁼u̲se< "Ffi" 11 12⍝# au:o_pen path: an audio file (Ogg Vorbis, MP3 or WAV); its id. 13"o_pen : text -> int" ᶠᶠⁱb̲ind< "audio/open"
