libraryextensions/blender/demos/CosyLogo.xtl

CosyLogo.xtl -- the CoSy logo as a Blender scene: the letters C, o, S, y as round tubes, toothpaste style, fused where they touch, glossy magenta on yellow, turned once round. Each letter is its center line, an array of points traced from the logo (pixels, y down); X_eTaL places them in meters, and Blender gives each line a round tube and round ends, then melts the tubes into one mesh. Shared by the demo (demos/cosy.xtl: the video) and its test (a tiny still). Import: "cy:" u_se< "CosyLogo"
