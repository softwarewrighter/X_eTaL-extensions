libraryextensions/blender/demos/HexBuild.xtl

HexBuild.xtl -- the HEX1000-UART build, from the pinned circuit's actual synthesis, placement and routing artifacts. X_eTaL computes the geometry, colors and timing; Video writes the Blender player. The board is a schematic model; die coordinates are nextpnr tiles, not physical dimensions. Routes show occupied wire tiles. Import: "hb:" u_se< "HexBuild"
