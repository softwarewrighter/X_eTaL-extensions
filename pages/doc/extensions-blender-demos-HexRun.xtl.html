libraryextensions/blender/demos/HexRun.xtl

HexRun.xtl -- HEX1000 computing C0DE+BEEF=, a video made with the explainer kit (Video.xtl) from a simulation of its board: the text sent over the serial line bit by bit, the board queuing each key, the machine scanning its key matrix, its program counter hopping through a ROM page, A, the status, the ALU and the RAM changing, and the digits strobed out to a calculator's display. Everything moving is a signal of the simulation's waveform (scripts/hex1000-sim.sh: the board's Verilog from X_eTaL-fpga at FPGA_COMMIT under iverilog; Vcd.xtl reads it), sampled once a frame; X_eTaL-fpga's Hex1000.xtl replays the same clocks from the K inputs the circuit saw and must agree with its registers at every one, and the display it ends with must be the evaluator's (hx:c_alc) -- else no script is written. Beside the calculator at the end, the browser calculator's screen (scripts/hex1000-browser.py). Shared by the demo (demos/hex-run.xtl) and its test. Import: "hr:" u_se< "HexRun"
