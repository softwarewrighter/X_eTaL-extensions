libraryextensions/blender/demos/Matmul.xtl

Matmul.xtl -- a matrix product, as two videos (Video.xtl): a small one a step at a time, C = A x B for X_eTaL-gpu's matmul demo ([3 4] x [4 2]), and a large one as a fly-through, 512 x 512 x 512. The products and sums are X_eTaL-gpu's Accel library (ac:m_atmul, the inner product its GPU runs); what is new here is only how they are shown: every cell's place, color and timing, every label and the camera's path, worked out from those arrays. Shared by the demos (demos/matmul-steps.xtl, demos/matmul-fly.xtl), their tests and the literate document (docs/literate/matmul.org). Import: "mm:" u_se< "Matmul"
