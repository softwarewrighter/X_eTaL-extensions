libraryextensions/blender/demos/MatmulFly.xtl

MatmulFly.xtl -- a large matrix product as a 3D construct, a fly-through video (Video.xtl): C = A x B for n by n matrices (512 in the demo), by X_eTaL-gpu's Accel library (ac:m_atmul). A stands on the left wall, B on the ceiling, C on the front wall, every item a cell colored by its value; the n x n x n multiply-adds fill the cube between them. The camera starts far out, flies to one work-group (a 16 by 16 tile of C, as a GPU schedules it), then to one work-item (a row of A times a column of B): its n products stand as bars, added one at a time from the far end into one item of C; then C fills in, a work-group at a time. Every array here is worked out in X_eTaL; Video.xtl writes them into the script Blender runs. Shared by the demo (demos/matmul-fly.xtl), its test and the literate document (docs/literate/matmul.org). Import: "mf:" u_se< "MatmulFly"
