libraryextensions/blender/demos/NeonCity.xtl

NeonCity.xtl -- a city at night after rain, worked out as arrays and ray-traced (Cycles.xtl): an avenue running north between two walls of towers, six lots deep on each side, every lot's tower a few rows of a table -- a body, a narrower tier, sometimes a spire -- with a height, a footprint and a skin of its own from a hash of its number; screens on the walls that face the avenue, bands of neon, signs, lamps, steam, bridges across, cars on the ground and in the air, and a camera that comes down from the skyline to the puddles. Nothing is drawn by hand and nothing is loaded: change nc:seed and it is another city. Shared by the demo (demos/city.xtl: stills), its preview and film, and its test. Import: "nc:" u_se< "NeonCity"
