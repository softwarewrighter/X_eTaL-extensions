libraryextensions/blender/demos/PcWalk.xtl

PcWalk.xtl -- HEX1000's program counter, a video made with the explainer kit (Video.xtl): the 64 addresses of a ROM page as cells in reading order, and the program counter visiting them in the order its shift register steps -- 0 1 3 7 15 31 63 62 ..., never +1 -- each cell lit as it is reached, colored by when, the path drawn behind it. The order is X_eTaL-fpga's Hex1000 library (hx:Order, pinned at FPGA_COMMIT); the first hops are in slow motion and the rest a time-lapse (vd:r_etime), behind a title card, with captions, a cue sheet and subtitles; the same scene fits a YouTube video (1920 by 1080) or a Short (1080 by 1920). Shared by the demo (demos/hex-pc.xtl) and its test. Import: "pw:" u_se< "PcWalk"

source · imports hx: work/fpga/libs/Hex1000/src/Hex1000.xtl; vd: extensions/blender/lib/Video.xtl
