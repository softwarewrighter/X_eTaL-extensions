libraryextensions/blender/demos/ReluSoftmax.xtl

ReluSoftmax.xtl -- ReLU and softmax on a tiny network's arrays, as a video (Video.xtl). The network is X_eTaL-gpu's tiny-net (libs/Accel/demos/tiny-net.xtl), run with the same library the GPU runs, Accel (X_eTaL-gpu at GPU_COMMIT, on XETAL_PATH by scripts/libraries.sh): its dense layers, ReLU, row maximum, row sums and softmax. What is new here is only how they are shown: every bar's place, size, color and timing, every label and the camera's path, worked out from those arrays, and Video.xtl writes them into the script Blender runs. Shared by the demo (demos/relu-softmax.xtl: the video), its test (a few small frames) and the literate document (docs/literate/relu-softmax.org). Import: "rs:" u_se< "ReluSoftmax"
