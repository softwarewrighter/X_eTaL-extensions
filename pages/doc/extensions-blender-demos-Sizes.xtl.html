Sizes.xtl -- the size comparison, xkcd style, as a Blender scene: one cube, a 10 by 10 slab of a hundred, a 10 by 10 by 10 block of a thousand, side by side on the ground and labeled, the camera going once round them. Shared by the demo (demos/cubes.xtl: the video) and its test (a few tiny frames). Import: "sz:" u_se< "Sizes"
