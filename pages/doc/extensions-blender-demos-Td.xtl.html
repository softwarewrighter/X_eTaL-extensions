libraryextensions/blender/demos/Td.xtl

Td.xtl -- the tower defense pieces of X_eTaL-games, described in X_eTaL as Blender parts (Bpy.xtl): three towers at three levels -- arrow, cannon, frost -- and three creeps -- runner, brute, shield. Each piece stands on a 1 by 1 tile at z 0, its front (and the part that aims) toward -y, a tower's base about 0.8 wide, a creep's about 0.5; flat colors, the frost crystals glowing. Each has its clips: idle (2 s, a loop) for all, fire (0.4 s) for towers, walk (1 s, a loop) and hit (0.3 s) for creeps. A part that moves in a clip hangs in a group (bp:g_roup), so it turns about its own pivot. Import: "td:" u_se< "Td"
