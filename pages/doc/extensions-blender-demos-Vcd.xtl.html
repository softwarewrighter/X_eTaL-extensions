libraryextensions/blender/demos/Vcd.xtl

Vcd.xtl -- a simulation's waveform (a VCD file, as iverilog writes it) read into arrays: every change of every signal as a row of numbers, and any signal's value at any times. Values wider than 62 bits do not fit an Int (split them into narrower signals); x and z read as 0. Used by the hex-run video (HexRun.xtl). Import: "vc:" u_se< "Vcd"
