programextensions/blender/demos/cubes.xtl

Blender, 2: how big is a thousand? One cube, a hundred, a thousand, side by side -- xkcd style -- rendered by Blender from a script X_eTaL writes: every cube's corners and faces are X_eTaL arrays, written into the script as numbers (Bpy.xtl, Sizes.xtl). The camera goes once round them; the video loops. Blender also writes the scene as files to open elsewhere: sizes.glb (glTF), sizes.obj and sizes.blend. Run: just demo blender cubes (about a minute or two: 240 frames)
