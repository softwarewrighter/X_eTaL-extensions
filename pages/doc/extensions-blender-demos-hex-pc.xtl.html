programextensions/blender/demos/hex-pc.xtl

Blender, 9: HEX1000's program counter, an explainer made with the kit. The 64 addresses of a ROM page as cells in reading order; the program counter visits them in its shift register's order -- 0 1 3 7 15 31 63 62 ..., never +1 -- each cell lit as it is reached, colored by when, the path drawn behind. The order is X_eTaL-fpga's Hex1000 library (pinned at FPGA_COMMIT); the first hops in slow motion, the rest a time-lapse, after a title card, with captions. Made twice -- a YouTube video (1920 by 1080) and a Short (1080 by 1920) -- with a cue sheet and subtitles (.srt) to record narration against (PcWalk.xtl). Run: just demo blender hex-pc (a few minutes: 586 frames, twice)
