programextensions/blender/demos/hex-run.xtl

Blender, 10: HEX1000-UART computing C0DE+BEEF=, from a simulation of its board. scripts/hex1000-sim.sh runs X_eTaL-fpga's board Verilog (at FPGA_COMMIT) under iverilog, the text sent over its serial line, into a waveform (VCD); scripts/hex1000-browser.py types the same text at the live browser calculator in headless Chrome. This program reads the waveform (Vcd.xtl), checks it -- the line carries the text, the board receives it, Hex1000.xtl replaying the clocks agrees with the circuit's registers at every one, the display ends as the evaluator's (hx:c_alc) and the browser's -- and only then writes the video (HexRun.xtl): the bits arriving and the key matrix in slow motion, the typing as a time-lapse, one addition in slow motion, the display, the browser's screen beside it. A YouTube video and a Short, with a cue sheet and subtitles. Title: HEX1000-UART: execution Command: just ext blender hex-run Run: just ext blender hex-run (the simulation, the browser, then this: about 25 minutes, 1261 frames twice)
