programextensions/blender/demos/matmul-fly.xtl

Blender, 7: a 512 x 512 matrix product as a 3D construct, a fly-through, arrays to video. A on the left wall, B on the ceiling, C on the front wall, every item a cell colored by its value; the camera starts far out (the whole cube: 134 million multiply-adds), flies to one work-group's 16 by 16 tile, then one work-item -- a row of A times a column of B, 512 products added into one item of C -- and back out while C fills in, a work-group at a time. The product is X_eTaL-gpu's Accel ac:m_atmul; every place, color and time is an array worked out in X_eTaL (demos/MatmulFly.xtl); Video.xtl writes them into the script Blender runs. Explained in docs/literate/matmul.org. Run: just demo blender matmul-fly (several minutes: 541 frames)
