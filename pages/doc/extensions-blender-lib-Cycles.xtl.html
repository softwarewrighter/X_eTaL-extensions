libraryextensions/blender/lib/Cycles.xtl

Cycles.xtl -- arrays to a ray-traced film: a Blender scene for Cycles (path tracing: real reflections, shadows from every light, light scattered in fog) described by X_eTaL arrays. A program works out the scene as tables -- a row a box, a lamp, a vehicle, a puff of steam -- and each function here writes one table into Blender's Python as numbers, one call of the player for a whole group. The player (cy:head) is fixed Python, the same for every film: it reads the numbers and makes the objects, materials and keys, and decides nothing. A film is cy:head, then cy:s_tage, the materials, the groups that wear them, lights, fog, the camera's flight, then cy:s_till, or cy:f_rames and cy:e_ncode; written with bl:w_rite! and run by bp:launcher through bl:r_un!.

Nothing is loaded: every surface is worked out by its material from where it is -- a facade's windows from the wall's own coordinates, a street's puddles from noise, a screen's picture from its face and the time -- so a table of boxes is a city. (cy:r_eel is the one exception: a screen playing a video file.)

Places are meters, z up; times are seconds; angles are degrees; colors are 0 to 255 (sRGB), made linear here; a light's strength is watts. Written for Blender 5.1. Import: "cy:" u_se< "Cycles"
