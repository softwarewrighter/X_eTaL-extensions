libraryextensions/blender/lib/Video.xtl

Video.xtl -- arrays to video: an animated Blender scene described by X_eTaL arrays. Every object's place, size, color, the seconds it is shown, every key it moves to, the camera's path and the captions are arrays a program works out; each function here writes them into Blender's Python as numbers, one call of the player for a whole group. The player (vd:head) is fixed Python, the same for every video: it reads the numbers and makes the objects and keys, and decides nothing. A video is vd:head, then vd:s_tage, the groups, their spans, keys and tints, the camera, the captions, then vd:r_ender (or vd:s_tills), written with bl:w_rite! and run by bp:launcher through bl:r_un!.

For explainer videos: the picture's size is any (vd:landscape, 1920 by 1080, or vd:shorts, 1080 by 1920, upright); vd:f_it places the camera so a region fills either; vd:r_etime remaps a scene's own seconds to the video's (slow motion, time-lapse); vd:g_lows keys how strongly things glow from data; vd:c_ards are title cards; captions and cards are sized to the picture and wrapped to its width; and vd:c_ues and vd:s_rt write the cue sheet and the subtitles (.srt) of the same captions, to record narration against.

The look: flat colors that glow (emission only), on a near-black sky, the Standard view (colors as given); text in JuliaMono Black (work/fonts, just font), facing the camera; captions on a dark plate near the bottom of the picture. Places are meters, z up; times are seconds; colors are 0 to 255 (sRGB), made linear here. Written for Blender 5.1. Import: "vd:" u_se< "Video"
