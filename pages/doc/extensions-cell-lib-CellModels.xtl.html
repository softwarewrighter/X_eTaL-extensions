libraryextensions/cell/lib/CellModels.xtl

CellModels.xtl -- the assembly cell's 3D models, described in X_eTaL and built by Blender: the arm (its pivots from the simulator, one node a joint, per the asset contract of docs/cell-design.md), the eight tools, the tool rack, a parts tray, the rotary stage with its vise, the microscope on its gantry, and every part kind with a node a pin. Each model is NAME.glb with NAME.rig.json beside it; a check reads every .glb back against its rig. Generic shapes, imitating no product. Millimeters and degrees; a tool's z is its approach, its mount at 0 (the flange), its tip at tool_tip. The Python is Bpy.xtl's head (bp:head) and the cell's builders, extensions/cell/models/cell_models.py; the calls are written here. Import: "cm:" u_se< "CellModels"
