libraryextensions/cell/lib/CellPlan.xtl

CellPlan.xtl -- the assembly cell's planner (decision C9, the slicer): a netlist as text to a command program for the cell. The core part goes in the vise; each neighbor is brought dead-bug to its core pin, coming in from outside along the pin (fanned a little along the pin row, so neighbors on one side spread apart), pinched by the turntable, soldered at the iron station and let go. The placement is worked out as arrays: the core's pin tips (ce:t_ips) turned into the cell, each neighbor's direction, where its body comes to rest, the clearance between every two bodies, and the order: one side of the core at a time, so the turntable's two pincers keep to their own sides. Import: "cp:" u_se< "CellPlan"

A netlist, a line each (# begins a comment):

  circuit plan          the circuit whose tray holds the parts
  core U1 dip8          the core: its id and kind
  R1 U1 2               R1's lead 1 to the core's pin 2

Each neighbor is an axial part (a resistor, a diode) joined by its lead 1; its body comes to rest 31 mm out from the joint.
