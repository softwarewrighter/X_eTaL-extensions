programextensions/image/demos/photo-lab.xtl

The photo lab: a photo is an array. Buzz Aldrin on the Moon (Apollo 11, NASA, public domain: demos/data/PROVENANCE.txt) read as numbers; filters and edges by rotating the array (the Life idiom), a color toning by an inner product, and the SVD compressing it at several ranks -- the native code reads, writes and decomposes, X_eTaL does the rest. Pictures go to work/photo-lab/. Run: just demo image photo-lab
