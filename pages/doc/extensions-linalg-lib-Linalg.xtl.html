libraryextensions/linalg/lib/Linalg.xtl

Linalg.xtl -- the facade of the linalg extension: numerical linear algebra through nalgebra. Matrices are Float arrays (row by column); a right-hand side may be a vector or a matrix of columns. Import: "la:" u_se< "Linalg" Run with xetal-x (xetal-x --ext DIR puts this directory on XETAL_PATH). Each signature line is written out by the binding macro (lib/Ffi.xtlm).
