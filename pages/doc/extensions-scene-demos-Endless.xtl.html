libraryextensions/scene/demos/Endless.xtl

Endless.xtl -- the endless world and its player, shared by the voxel demos that walk or fly in it (voxels-endless, voxels-fly, and those after): columns of 16 by 16 blocks made around the player and dropped behind it, each made over three frames so no frame waits long, and the player -- walking, swimming, flying -- moving against them. Import: "en:" u_se< "Endless"
