libraryextensions/scene/demos/RubikPlay.xtl

RubikPlay.xtl -- playing the voxel Rubik's cube (Rubik.xtl) with buttons and keys, shared by voxels-rubik-buttons and voxels-rubik-solve: a row of labeled buttons drawn over the scene and found under a click, and turns queued and animated one after another -- each a quarter turn of one layer seen as it happens, the next starting as soon as it is done, so a press always answers at once. Import: "rp:" u_se< "RubikPlay"
