programextensions/scene/demos/voxels-dig.xtl

Voxels, 10: digging and building in the endless world. A crosshair marks where you look; the block under it, within 5 blocks, is found by marching 100 points along the view ray (en:p_ick). Click it to dig it out; right-click to place a block of the kind chosen on the hotbar against it. Each change is an edit the world remembers (x y z and the block), and every column is made with its edits -- the edited one made again at once (and its neighbor, when the block is on their border), so walk away and come back and the hole is still there. Run: just demo scene voxels-dig click (or X) dig, right-click (or E) place, 1 to 6 choose the block (stone dirt grass sand wood leaves), W A S D walk, Space jump, Shift run, F fly (Space up, Shift down), drag or the arrows to look, H back to the start, q quit
