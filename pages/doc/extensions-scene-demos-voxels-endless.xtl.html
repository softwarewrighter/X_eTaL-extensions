programextensions/scene/demos/voxels-endless.xtl

Voxels, 6: an endless world. Walk in any direction and it never ends: the land comes from value noise hashed from the coordinates (vx:h_eightsAt), so any column of 16 by 16 blocks can be made by itself and fits its neighbors; columns are made around the player as it walks -- the nearest missing one each frame -- and dropped behind it; fog hides the edge of what is made, and a curved horizon (scene lowers the world by d^2 / 2R) makes it look like a small planet. Collision asks the columns themselves. The world and the player are the library Endless.xtl, shared with voxels-fly: each column is made over three frames, so no frame waits long. Run: just demo scene voxels-endless W A S D walk, Shift run, Space jump (in water: swim up; at the surface: climb out onto a bank one block high), drag the mouse or the arrows to look, H: back to the start, q or close: quit
