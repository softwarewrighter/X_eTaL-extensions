programextensions/scene/demos/voxels-faces.xtl

Voxels, 2: which faces to draw. A face shows where a solid block's neighbor is not solid: six rotations of the solid mask, each compared with the mask itself (the idiom of Life), give every exposed face of the chunk at once; the faces become a list, one row each: x y z direction block. Checked on shapes with known answers, then the chunk's faces drawn as outlines in a 3D window, turning. Run: just demo scene voxels-faces (drag to turn it; q or close: quit)
