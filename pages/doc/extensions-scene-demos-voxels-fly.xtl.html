programextensions/scene/demos/voxels-fly.xtl

Voxels, 7: flying. The endless world of voxels-endless, now from the air: F switches between walking and flying. Flying moves level whatever you look at -- W forward, S back, A and D sideways -- Space rises and Shift sinks, with no gravity, the blocks still stopping you; so you can look down at the land as you cross it. F again drops you back to walking wherever you are. The columns are made ahead, one a frame, and dropped behind, under fog and a curved horizon. Run: just demo scene voxels-fly F fly or walk; flying: W A S D level, Space up, Shift down; walking: Space jump or swim up, Shift run; drag the mouse or the arrows to look, H: back to the start, q quit
