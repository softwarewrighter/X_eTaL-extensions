programextensions/scene/demos/voxels-game.xtl

Voxels, 15: Gem Hunt. Ten gems are buried around the start, a few blocks under the surface; dig them out in three minutes. The first lies four blocks straight down from where you start; the others are scattered within a dozen blocks, deeper. Gems glow a little (12 where a lamp is 15), so a tunnel near one is lit -- and the corner of the screen says how far the nearest gem you have not found is. Everything from the voxel demos works: dig, place, lamps, water, night. G gives up and shows the score. The game is X_eTaL: the gems are edits, a find is a dig whose cell is a gem's, the clock is the frames (60 a second), the labels are scene's. Run: just demo scene voxels-game click (or X) dig, right-click (or E) place, 1 to 8 choose the block (8 a lamp), N night or day, W A S D walk, Space jump, F fly, drag or the arrows to look, G give up, H back to the start, q quit
