programextensions/scene/demos/voxels-light.xtl

Voxels, 14: light. The endless world, lit: a face open to the sky is in full sunlight; under an overhang, beneath a tree's crown or down a tunnel the light fades, a step a block, as it spreads from the open air. A lamp (the hotbar's eighth block) glows: its light spreads the same way through the air around it. N turns day to night and back -- the sunlight and the sky go, the lamps stay -- by one number in scene, so nothing is worked out again. Each column's light is worked out as it is made (vx:c_olumnLight: sunlight by a scan down each column, then rounds of spreading), and again when an edit makes it again: dig a tunnel's roof open and the sun comes in. Run: just demo scene voxels-light click (or X) dig, right-click (or E) place, 1 to 8 choose the block (8 a lamp), N night or day, W A S D walk, Space jump, Shift run, F fly, drag or the arrows to look, H back to the start, q quit
