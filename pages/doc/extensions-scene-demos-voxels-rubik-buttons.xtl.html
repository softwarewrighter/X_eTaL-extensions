programextensions/scene/demos/voxels-rubik-buttons.xtl

Voxels, 12: the Rubik's cube with buttons. Six buttons under the cube turn its top, right and front layers a quarter turn either way -- U and U' the top, R and R' the right, F and F' the front (a letter alone clockwise as you look at that face, with ' counterclockwise), which between them reach every position of the cube. Each press is animated, a quarter turn in 8 frames; presses made while a turn is under way wait their turn, so the cube answers at once and never drops one. The turns made are shown above the cube. Run: just demo scene voxels-rubik-buttons click a button, or the keys u r f (Shift for '), z undo the last turn, 0 solved again, drag to look around, q quit
