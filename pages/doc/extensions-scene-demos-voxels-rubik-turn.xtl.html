programextensions/scene/demos/voxels-rubik-turn.xtl

Voxels, 9: a quarter turn, seen. One layer of the Rubik's cube -- 9 cubies, a "plane" -- turns a quarter revolution while the rest stays still: its cubies' bodies and stickers rotated a little more each frame (a rotation matrix, one inner product), then the cube's colors permuted once it is done. Three orthogonal planes in turn, each a quarter turn one way and back: the top (U, U'), the right (R, R'), the front (F, F'). Run: just demo scene voxels-rubik-turn (drag to turn it; q quit)
