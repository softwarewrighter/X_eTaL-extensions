programextensions/scene/demos/voxels-rubik-turn.xtl

Voxels, 9: a quarter turn, seen. One layer of the Rubik's cube -- 9 cubies, a "plane" -- turns a quarter revolution while the rest stays still: its cubies' bodies and stickers rotated a little more each frame (a rotation matrix, one inner product), then the cube's colors permuted once it is done. A scramble: twelve quarter turns, each on a different axis from the one before (the faces in the order U R F D L B, twice), the directions + + - - + - and then - + + + - +; a pause; then the same turns undone in reverse, back to solved. Run: just demo scene voxels-rubik-turn (drag to turn it; q quit)
