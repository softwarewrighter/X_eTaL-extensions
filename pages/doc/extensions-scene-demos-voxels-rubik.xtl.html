programextensions/scene/demos/voxels-rubik.xtl

Voxels, 8: a Rubik's cube. 26 dark cubies with their colored stickers, drawn as voxels; the cube itself is arrays (Rubik.xtl): its 54 stickers, each where it is and which way it faces, and each of the twelve quarter turns a permutation of them, computed from the cube's geometry. This demo checks the turns -- each a permutation, four of one solved, a turn and its undoing solved, R U R' U' of order 6, R U of order 105, and U and R turning the right way -- and shows the solved cube, turning slowly; keys turn its faces. Run: just demo scene voxels-rubik u d r l f b turn a face clockwise (as you look at it), Shift with the letter counterclockwise, z undo (back to solved), Space a scramble (25 random turns), 0 reset, drag to look around, q quit
