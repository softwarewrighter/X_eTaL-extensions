programextensions/scene/demos/voxels-walk.xtl

Voxels, 5: walking. The island of voxels-world, seen from inside it. The player is nine numbers -- x y z (feet), vx vy vz, yaw, pitch, on the ground -- and every frame X_eTaL reads the keys and the mouse, turns, falls, jumps, and moves one axis at a time against the solid blocks the player's box would overlap, stopping at the face of the block it hits (a swept box per axis, as the APL game collides); then it sets scene's first-person camera. It also tests the 32 chunks against the planes of the view, as the APL game culls (key c). Run: just demo scene voxels-walk (a new island each run; --seed 1) W A S D walk, Shift run, Space jump (in water: swim up; at the surface: climb out onto a bank one block high), drag the mouse or the arrows to look, c: the chunks in view, H: back to the start, q or close: quit
