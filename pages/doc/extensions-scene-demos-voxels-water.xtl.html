programextensions/scene/demos/voxels-water.xtl

Voxels, 11: water that flows. A stepped hill stands by the start with a lake on its top terrace, held in by a rim of grass. Dig into the rim and the lake spills out and runs down the steps like a waterfall; dig beside the sea and the hole fills. Water flows as a cellular automaton on the cells it has just reached (Endless.xtl): each tick a cell falls into the air below it, or, standing on something solid, spreads one level lower into the air at its sides, 7 levels from a source; the lake and the sea are sources that never empty. The cells it reaches are edits like any dug or placed block, so the water stays where it ran. Water is drawn translucent: the lake's floor and the seabed show through. Run: just demo scene voxels-water click (or X) dig, right-click (or E) place, 1 to 7 choose the block (7 is water, which flows), W A S D walk, Space jump, Shift run, F fly (Space up, Shift down), drag or the arrows to look, H back to the rim, q quit
