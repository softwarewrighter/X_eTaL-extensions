librarystd/Stats.xtl

Stats: a small statistics library (a standard library, built into xetal). Import it with an alias of your choice: "s:" u_se< "Stats". It is a demo library, loaded only by that import: a small subset of X_eTaL-libraries' Statistics, which has much more (a program may use either). Names with l: are exported; names with h: are private to this file.
